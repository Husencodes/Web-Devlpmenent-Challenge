    • Gulab Jamun

    Ingredients:-

    • 1 cup milk powder
    • ¼ cup all-purpose flour (maida)
    • 2 tablespoons ghee
    • ¼ cup milk
    • ½ teaspoon baking soda
    • Oil or ghee for deep frying
    • 1½ cups sugar
    • 1½ cups water
    • 3–4 cardamom pods
    • 1 teaspoon rose water (optional)
    • A few saffron strands (optional)

    Procedure

    1. Prepare the sugar syrup: Add sugar and water to a pan and boil until the sugar completely dissolves. Add cardamom and rose water. Keep the syrup warm.
    2. Prepare the dough: Mix milk powder, maida, and baking soda in a bowl. Add ghee and mix well. Gradually add milk and gently combine to make a soft dough.
    3. Make the balls: Grease your hands and make small, smooth balls without cracks.
    4. Deep fry: Heat oil or ghee on low-medium heat. Fry the balls slowly until they become golden brown.
    5. Soak: Remove the fried gulab jamuns and immediately place them in the warm sugar syrup.
    6. Let them soak for 2–3 hours so they become soft and juicy.
    7. Serve: Serve warm or chilled, optionally garnished with pistachios or almonds.

    Gobi

    Ingredients

    1. 1 medium cauliflower (gobi)
    2. ½ cup all-purpose flour (maida)
    3. ¼ cup corn flour
    4. 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
    5. 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
    6. Salt to taste
    7. Water as needed
    8. Oil for deep frying
    9. 1 tablespoon oil
    10. 1 onion, chopped
    11. 1 capsicum, chopped
    12. 2 green chillies, chopped
    13. 1 tablespoon soy sauce
    14. 1 tablespoon tomato ketchup
    15. 1 teaspoon chilli sauce
    16. 1 teaspoon vinegar
    17. 2 tablespoons spring onions

    👨‍🍳 Procedure

    1. Prepare the gobi: Cut the cauliflower into small florets and wash them thoroughly. Boil water with a little salt and blanch the gobi for 2–3 minutes. Drain well.
    2. Prepare the batter: Mix maida, corn flour, red chilli powder, ginger-garlic paste, and salt. Add water and make a medium-thick batter.
    3. Dip the gobi florets into the batter and coat them evenly.
    4. Deep fry: Heat oil and fry the coated gobi until crispy and golden brown. Remove and keep aside.
    5. Prepare the sauce: Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a pan. Add onion, capsicum, and green chillies. Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
    6. Add soy sauce, tomato ketchup, chilli sauce, and vinegar. Mix well.
    7. Add the fried gobi and toss everything together until well coated.
    8. Garnish: Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.

    Mysore siri

    Ingredients:-

      • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
      • 1 cup sugar
      • 1 cup ghee
      • ½ cup water
      • 2 tablespoons oil
      • ¼ teaspoon cardamom powder
      • A few cashew nuts (optional)

    👨‍🍳 Procedure

    1. Prepare the besan: Sieve the besan to remove any lumps.
    2. Make the sugar syrup: Add sugar and water to a pan and cook until a slightly sticky syrup forms.
    3. Add besan: Gradually add the besan while stirring continuously to prevent lumps.
    4. Add ghee: Slowly add warm ghee in small portions and keep stirring.
    5. Continue cooking until the mixture becomes thick and starts leaving the sides of the pan.
    6. Add cardamom powder and mix well.
    7. Set the Mysore Pak: Grease a tray with ghee and transfer the mixture into it. Spread evenly.
    8. Allow it to cool for a few minutes, then cut into square or diamond-shaped pieces.
    9. Let it cool completely and serve delicious Mysore Pak.

    Cake

    Ingredients:-

    1. 1½ cups all-purpose flour (maida)
    2. 1 cup sugar
    3. ½ cup butter
    4. 2 eggs
    5. ½ cup milk
    6. 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
    7. 1 teaspoon baking powder
    8. ½ teaspoon baking soda
    9. A pinch of salt

    👨‍🍳 Procedure

    1. Prepare the batter: Mix butter and sugar in a bowl until light and creamy.
    2. Add the eggs one at a time and mix well. Add vanilla essence and mix.
    3. In another bowl, combine maida, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
    4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, alternating with milk. Mix gently until smooth.
    5. Prepare the pan: Grease a cake pan with butter and lightly dust it with flour. Pour the batter into the pan and spread it evenly.
    6. Bake: Preheat the oven to 180°C and bake for about 30–35 minutes.
    7. Check with a toothpick. If it comes out clean, the cake is ready.
    8. Allow the cake to cool, then cut into slices and serve. 🍰

    Gobi

    Ingredients

    1. 1 cup cashew nuts (kaju)
    2. ½ cup sugar
    3. ¼ cup water
    4. ½ teaspoon cardamom powder
    5. 1 teaspoon ghee
    6. Silver vark (optional)

    👨‍🍳 Procedure

    1. Prepare the cashews: Grind the cashews into a fine powder without making it oily.
    2. Make the sugar syrup: Add sugar and water to a pan and cook until the sugar dissolves and forms a slightly sticky syrup.
    3. Add the cashew powder gradually and stir continuously on low heat.
    4. Cook until the mixture becomes thick and starts leaving the sides of the pan.
    5. Add cardamom powder and ghee, then mix well.
    6. Prepare the dough: Transfer the mixture onto a greased surface and allow it to cool slightly. Knead gently until it becomes smooth.
    7. Roll it into a thin, even layer using a rolling pin.
    8. Cut into diamond-shaped pieces.
    9. Add silver vark if desired and serve Kaju Katli. 🍬
